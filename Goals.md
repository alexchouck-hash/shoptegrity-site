# Shoptegrity: Mission, Directives & Goals

> **Core Thesis:** The modern economy functions as a massive wealth siphon, funneling capital away from workers, farmers, and communities directly into the hands of the top 10% (and top 1%). Shoptegrity exists to make money flow visible, expose corporate deception, and deliberately redirect capital into the hands of the other 90%—domestically and internationally—while demanding real ecological stewardship for people, plants, and animals.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Foundational Purpose

Every time a consumer spends a dollar, they cast an economic vote. Today, that vote is systematically manipulated:
- **Extreme Financial Concentration:** In the United States, the top 10% of households own roughly 90% of corporate equities and mutual funds. Globally, between 95% and 99% of Wall Street asset-management holdings belong exclusively to the global top 10%. The bottom 90% of humanity produces the world's real value, yet receives only a tiny trickle of the financial returns.
- **Corporate Camouflage & Subterfuge:** Conglomerates and private equity platforms quietly acquire independent, natural, and local businesses, maintaining rustic labels and family names to trick ethical shoppers into funding the very monopolies they wish to avoid.
- **The Greenwashing Epidemic:** Billions of dollars are poured into glossy sustainability reports, 2050 carbon pledges, and token philanthropic donations (e.g., donating 1% while extracting billions through stock buybacks, worker underpayment, and ecological degradation).

**Shoptegrity's standard is uncompromising:**
A brand has value solely because of what it **does**, never what it **says**. The most harmful brands are those that claim moral high ground while practicing covert extraction. Real impact is measured in **where the cash ends up** and **how the land, living creatures, and working people are treated**.

## 2. Strategic Goals & Directives

### Goal 1: Reroute Capital to the 90% (Domestic & Global)
Our primary economic directive is to shift the terminal destination of daily spending away from the top 10% and into the hands of the 90%.

* **Domestic Flow:** Enable households to bypass the megacorp and private equity pipelines. Show consumers how switching everyday services (banking to local credit unions, grocery to farmer-owned co-ops, home services to true independent owner-operators) keeps capital circulating among working families.
* **International Equity:** The 90% is global. Over 70% of the world's population lives on less than $10,000 in net assets. When purchasing internationally sourced commodities (coffee, tea, cocoa, textiles, spices, produce), Shoptegrity prioritizes **direct-export producer cooperatives, smallholder collectives, and transparent direct-trade relationships** that pay living wealth directly to growers in the Global South, completely bypassing multinational commodity cartels.
* **Producer Dollar Retention:** In every consumer category, track the exact percentage of the purchase price that reaches the hands-on producer (e.g., the USDA food dollar split showing farmers receive only ~11.8¢ at standard supermarkets vs. 85¢+ via direct farm stands, CSAs, or producer co-ops).

### Goal 2: Strip Away Corporate Subterfuge & Hidden Ownership
Consumers cannot vote with their dollars if the ballot is fraudulent.

* **Exposing the Conglomerate Curtain:** Systematically reveal the true parent corporations behind beloved "boutique," "heritage," or "natural" brands (e.g., unmasking that seemingly independent natural food and personal care brands are owned by General Mills, Unilever, Clorox, or Nestlé).
* **The Private Equity Rollup Warning System:** Identify and flag disguised acquisitions in essential service industries—such as local HVAC, plumbing, veterinary clinics, funeral homes, and medical/dental practices—where private equity platforms buy local companies but deliberately retain the former owner's family name to deceive the neighborhood.
* **Radical Beneficial Ownership Tracking:** Ensure every brand, service, and product scorecard links directly to regulatory disclosures, SEC filings, and corporate registry records showing who actually takes home the net profit.

### Goal 3: Authentic Care for People, Plants, and Animals
Environmental stewardship is meaningless without direct accountability to living ecosystems.

* **Ethical Land Use:** Prioritize agricultural and industrial practices that restore soil health, protect watersheds, and minimize monoculture disruption. Support smallholders practicing rotational grazing, agroforestry, and low-tillage regenerative farming.
* **Natural Habitat Preservation:** Hold producers accountable to zero-deforestation, wildlife corridor preservation, and non-destructive harvesting. Champion brands that preserve wild biodiversity rather than converting intact ecosystems into industrial feedlots or monoculture plantations.
* **Humane Treatment of Animals & Workers:** Reject factory farming, feedlot abuse, and labor exploitation. True integrity treats animals with dignity, guarantees safe working conditions, and pays thriving, living wages to human laborers.

### Goal 4: Absolute Rejection of Lip Service & Performative Pledges
The market is saturated with empty marketing disguised as virtue. Shoptegrity enforces a strict filter against performative ethics:

* **Zero Credit for Future Pledges:** "Net-Zero by 2050" or "100% Recyclable by 2035" announcements earn **zero points** in our scoring rubric. A pledge is PR; only existing, verified practices receive credit.
* **Donations Are Not Restitution:** A multinational company that emits millions of tons of pollutants or underpays 50,000 workers cannot buy an ethical reputation by donating $1 million to an environmental non-profit. The core operating model must be clean.
* **Beware the "Token Donation" Trap:** Marketing campaigns highlighting micro-donations are often used to distract from macro-extraction. If 90% of a company's profits flow to offshore entities or executive stock buybacks, a small charitable donation will not offset its score.
* **Penalizing Hypocrisy:** Brands that explicitly market themselves as "green," "ethical," or "clean" while violating environmental regulations or hiding parent extraction are penalized more severely than transparent conventional brands.

### Goal 5: Prioritize Direct-to-Consumer Small Producers
The most effective way to guarantee money reaches the 90% is to shorten the supply chain until the middleman disappears.

* **Direct Maker & Grower First:** Elevate small-batch producers, family farms, community gardens, local craftspeople, and independent repairers who sell direct to the public.
* **Disintermediation:** Replace multi-tier wholesale/retail distribution with direct purchasing mechanisms (CSAs, farm stands, direct e-commerce, regional co-ops).
* **Affordability & True Value:** Dispel the myth that integrity is a luxury good. Highlight practical swaps that save money (buying used, borrowing/repairing, cutting delivery app markups, shifting to credit unions) alongside high-integrity direct goods.

## 3. Core Principles of the Shoptegrity Standard

```
         THE INTEGRITY PYRAMID
                /\
               /  \       Tier 1: Direct Makers, Growers & Worker Co-ops
              / T1 \      (100% of profit to the 90%; direct ecological care)
             /------\
            /   T2   \    Tier 2: Certified Employee-Owned & Member Mutuals
           /----------\   (Profits shared equitably among workers/members)
          /     T3     \  Tier 3: Independent Local Businesses (Transparent)
         /--------------\ (Money recirculates within regional communities)
        /       T4       \Tier 4: Conventional Corporations (Clear, honest footprint)
       /------------------\
      /         T5         \ Tier 5: Subterfuge & False Greenwashers
     /                      \ (Conglomerate-owned fronts, PE rollups, PR-pledge brands)
    --------------------------
```

1. **Receipts Over Claims:** If a claim cannot be verified through public regulatory records (EPA ECHO, OSHA, NLRB, SEC, USDA certifications, local registry audits), it does not exist.
2. **Follow the Cash:** The definitive question for every transaction is: *When $100 leaves the buyer's pocket, who deposits the net margin into their bank account?*
3. **Radical Transparency:** Our rubric, data sources, and calculations are completely open. No business can buy a rating, sponsor an audit, or pay to remove an ownership flag.

---

## 4. The Measure of Success

Shoptegrity succeeds when:
1. **Dollars Diverted:** Measurable consumer spending is diverted from multinational holding conglomerates and private equity rollups into local producers, co-ops, and direct growers.
2. **Deception Neutralized:** Consumers can instantly verify whether an artisanal brand or local service is genuinely independent before making a purchase.
3. **Living Wealth Created:** Small growers and ethical producers—both in our hometowns and in agricultural communities around the world—receive the full financial value of their labor, allowing their families, soil, and communities to thrive.
