Where Does Your Food Dollar Land?

According to the USDA Economic Research Service (ERS) Food Dollar Series, the farmer receives only 11.8¢ of every food dollar spent nationally—while shareholders and marketing take the lion's share.

⚡ The Extractive Reality: $100 Spent on Food Source: USDA ERS + SEC Form 10-K Filings
