Public Methodology & Integrity Firewall

Credibility is our only asset. We operate under a strict separation between scoring, citations, and funding. No brand can pay to alter, improve, or sponsor their score.

The Integrity Firewall Policy

Core Principle:

Scores are pure mathematical functions derived strictly from documented, publicly verifiable regulatory actions and financial filings. The scoring engine has zero read access to commercial or affiliate data.

  • No Paid Placements: We refuse sponsored brand listings, paid tier bumps, or brand consulting.
  • Evidence-Backed or Explicitly "Unknown": Private companies that refuse public disclosure are given an explicit "Unknown" rating—opacity is never rewarded.
  • Facts, Not Characters: Every claim quotes factual legal settlements, OSHA citations, or SEC line items rather than characterizations.
  • Public Corrections Log: Any company or citizen can submit documented factual corrections via our public intake queue. Validated corrections are deployed within 5 business days.
