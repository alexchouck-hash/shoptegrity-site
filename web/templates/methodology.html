Public Methodology & Integrity Firewall

Our policy: we keep scoring, citations, and commercial relationships separate, and no brand can pay to alter its score.

The Integrity Firewall Policy

Core Principle:

Grades are Shoptegrity's opinion, reached by applying this published rubric to cited facts. When a grade's facts are not cited, the grade is withheld rather than guessed.

  • No paid placements: Our policy: we do not accept sponsored brand listings, paid placements, or consulting from rated brands. Commercial relationships are listed on our disclosures page.
  • Withheld, not guessed: Where we cannot cite a fact, we say so. The item shows as Withheld: no cited source yet and is not presented as a fact.
  • Sourced or withheld: Our policy: a specific factual claim (a count, a rate, an enforcement action, an ownership link) is published only with a link to its source.
  • Corrections: Anyone can request a correction. Requests are reviewed before anything changes.

Where the Numbers Come From

  • SEC filings: For public companies, financial figures come from SEC XBRL data for a specific filing, which is linked. A figure is withheld when its filing cannot be retrieved.
  • Estimates: Our policy: we do not publish estimates about named companies. A model used to illustrate a point is labelled as an illustrative assumption, and its output is not a measurement.
  • Earlier index removed: Earlier versions showed a "top 10% ownership" index for brands. It was a formula, not company-level data, and it has been removed.
  • OSHA records: OSHA counts are published only after a reviewer approves the matched establishment names. Each published count links to OSHA's establishment search. Until that review is complete, no OSHA count is published.
  • Sourced facts: Regulatory and labor facts appear only with a "[source]" link to the regulator record, court filing, or report.
  • Ratings (opinion): Letter grades and the labor-risk and waste ratings are our opinions under this rubric. A rating is shown only when its inputs are cited.
