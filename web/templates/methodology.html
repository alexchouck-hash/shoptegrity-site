Public Methodology & Integrity Firewall

Credibility is our only asset. We operate under a strict separation between scoring, citations, and funding. No brand can pay to alter, improve, or sponsor their score.

The Integrity Firewall Policy

Core Principle:

Grades are Shoptegrity's opinion, reached by applying this published rubric to documented facts: ownership structure, SEC financial filings, and sourced regulatory records. The scoring engine has zero read access to commercial or affiliate data.

  • No Paid Placements: We refuse sponsored brand listings, paid tier bumps, or brand consulting.
  • Evidence-Backed or Explicitly "Unknown": Private companies that refuse public disclosure are given an explicit "Unknown" rating—opacity is never rewarded.
  • Sourced or Withheld: A specific factual claim (an OSHA count, a denial rate, an enforcement action) is published only with a link to its source. Claims we cannot yet source are withheld automatically, not shown.
  • Public Corrections: Any company or citizen can request a correction. We acknowledge within 2 business days and resolve within 10.

Where the Numbers Come From

  • SEC filings (reported): For public companies, revenue, net income, dividends paid, share buybacks, and CEO total compensation are extracted automatically from SEC EDGAR XBRL data for the latest fiscal year, with a link to the exact filing. "Shareholders" in the dollar split = (dividends + buybacks) ÷ revenue. "CEO pay" = CEO total compensation ÷ revenue.
  • Estimates (labelled "est."): The wage, marketing, and cost-of-goods shares of the dollar split are Shoptegrity estimates from company disclosures and sector data. They are not reported line items. "Operations & other" is whatever remains.
  • Top 10% Ownership Index (est.): An estimate of how much of a company's shareholder payout reaches the wealthiest 10% of households, based on Federal Reserve Distributional Financial Accounts data on stock ownership.
  • OSHA records: Violation counts come from the U.S. Department of Labor's OSHA enforcement data: citation items from inspections opened in a fixed five-year window at establishments matching the company's names, excluding withdrawn citations. A reviewer checks the matched establishment names before a count is published, and every count links to OSHA's own establishment search.
  • Sourced facts: Regulatory and labor facts appear only with a "[source]" link to the regulator record, court filing, or report.
  • Ratings (opinion): Letter grades and the labor, waste, and sweatshop-risk ratings are our assessments under this rubric. They are opinions, not statements of fact.
