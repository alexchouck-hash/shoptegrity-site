Sell Direct. Keep More of Each Sale.

When customers buy through a marketplace, the platform takes a cut of the order and holds the customer relationship. Your own online store keeps both with you.

Marketplace vs. Your Own Store

  • Marketplace fees: marketplaces can charge a referral fee on sales, plus optional advertising and fulfillment fees. Check each platform's current fee schedule, for example Amazon's seller pricing.
  • Your own store: you typically pay a monthly plan and payment-processing fees instead of a per-sale referral fee, and you keep the customer's contact details. Check the current plan terms before you decide.
  • Both: some producers keep a marketplace listing for discovery and point repeat customers to their own store.

Getting Started

  1. List your best sellers first, with clear photos and your story: who makes it, where, and how.
  2. Tell existing customers (farmers market, CSA, wholesale buyers) where to order online.
  3. Compare total costs before choosing a platform: monthly plan, payment processing, and any add-on apps.
