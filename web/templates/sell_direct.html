Sell Direct. Keep More of Each Sale.

When customers buy through a giant marketplace, the platform takes a cut of every order and owns the customer relationship. Your own online store puts that relationship, and more of the money, back with you.

Marketplace vs. Your Own Store

  • Marketplace fees: large marketplaces charge a referral fee on every sale, plus optional advertising and fulfillment fees. Check the current schedule, for example Amazon's seller pricing.
  • Your own store: you pay a monthly plan and payment processing instead of a per-sale referral cut, and you keep the customer's contact so you can sell to them again.
  • Best of both: many small producers keep a marketplace listing for discovery and send repeat customers to their own store.

Getting Started

  1. List your best sellers first, with clear photos and your story: who makes it, where, and how.
  2. Tell existing customers (farmers market, CSA, wholesale buyers) where to order online.
  3. Compare total costs before choosing a platform: monthly plan, payment processing, and any add-on apps.
