Vetted Data Sources of Better Brands

We cross-reference these official open registries to surface verified alternatives:

Worker & Producer Co-ops

Democratically owned by employees or regional agricultural producers. Profits recirculate rather than extract.

Sources: USFWC & National Co+op Grocers

100% Employee-Owned (ESOP)

100% of equity held in trust for staff (e.g. King Arthur Baking, Bob's Red Mill). Zero outside wall-street buybacks.

Source: National Center for Employee Ownership

Certified B Corporations

Legally required to balance profit and public benefit, audited on governance, worker pay, and environment.

Source: B Lab Global Directory

Member-Owned Credit Unions

Not-for-profit financial cooperatives chartered to serve depositors. 0% of margins fund fossil-fuel or hedge funds.

Source: NCUA Public Registry
