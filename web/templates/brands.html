How Shoptegrity Tiers Swaps: Best vs. Better

Changing shopping habits shouldn't be all-or-nothing. Brand pages may list two options:

⚡ BETTER SWAP Similar Price & Format

The pragmatic drop-in: an option aimed at a price and shopping experience close to the conventional brand. Tier ratings are Shoptegrity opinion (Grade B / B+).

🏆 BEST SWAP Deep Impact: Gold Standard

The gold standard: a higher-rated option, such as a worker or member cooperative or a purpose trust (Shoptegrity opinion, Grade A / A+). The format or experience may differ, and upfront effort or cost may be higher.

Ownership Structures Used in Our Swaps

These are definitions only. They do not describe any specific company. Ratings are Shoptegrity opinion.

Worker & Producer Co-ops

Owned and governed by their members: workers, shoppers or producers. Check each business's own governance documents.

Employee-Owned (ESOP)

An employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) holds company shares in a trust for staff. Confirm a company's status in its own filings or disclosures.

Certified B Corporations

A voluntary certification from B Lab. It is separate from state benefit-corporation status.
