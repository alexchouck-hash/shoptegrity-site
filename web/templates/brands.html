How Shoptegrity Tiers Swaps: Best vs. Better

Changing shopping habits shouldn't be all-or-nothing. Every brand scorecard in our database offers two vetted options: an accessible drop-in replacement that matches conventional price points, and a gold-standard ethical alternative for maximum community impact.

⚡ BETTER SWAP Easy: Similar Price & Format

The pragmatic drop-in: An accessible option (Grade B / B+) matching conventional pricing and familiar supermarket shelves or 1-click checkout. Low friction; cuts out the worst corporate abuses (anti-union retaliation, predatory debt, Wall Street buyback extraction).

🏆 BEST SWAP Deep Impact: Gold Standard

The gold standard: A high-scoring ethical brand, worker/member cooperative, or purpose trust (Grade A / A+). The format or experience may differ and upfront effort might be higher (bulk bins, farm CSAs, refill bottles), but keeps maximum wealth in the community.

Verified Data Sources for Ethical Swaps

Every alternative in our database is verified against public registries and regulatory filings:

Worker & Producer Co-ops

Democratically owned by employees or regional agricultural producers. Profits recirculate rather than extract to Wall Street.

Sources: USFWC & National Co+op Grocers

100% Employee-Owned (ESOP)

100% of equity held in trust for staff (e.g. WinCo Foods, King Arthur Baking). Zero outside hedge fund extraction.

Source: National Center for Employee Ownership

Certified B Corporations

Legally required to balance profit and public benefit, audited on governance, worker pay, and environment.

Source: B Lab Global Directory

Member-Owned Credit Unions

Not-for-profit financial cooperatives chartered to serve depositors. 0% of margins fund fossil-fuel or hedge funds.

Source: NCUA Public Registry
