How Shoptegrity Tiers Swaps: Best vs. Better

Changing shopping habits shouldn't be all-or-nothing. Every swap guide on Shoptegrity offers two tailored paths:

⚡ BETTER SWAP Similar Price & Format

The pragmatic drop-in: An okay-scoring option (Grade B / B+) that matches conventional price points and shopping convenience. Low friction; cuts out the worst abuses (anti-union crackdowns, predatory fees, PE rollups).

🏆 BEST SWAP High Ethical Standard

The gold standard: A high-scoring ethical brand, worker/member cooperative, or purpose trust (Grade A / A+). The format or experience may differ and upfront cost might be higher, but keeps maximum wealth in the community.
