How Shoptegrity Tiers Swaps: Best vs. Better

Changing shopping habits shouldn't be all-or-nothing. Every swap guide on Shoptegrity offers two tailored paths:

⚡ BETTER SWAP Similar Price & Format

The pragmatic drop-in: an option aimed at a price and shopping experience close to the conventional store. Tier ratings are Shoptegrity opinion (Grade B / B+).

🏆 BEST SWAP High Ethical Standard

The gold standard: a higher-rated option, such as a worker or member cooperative or a purpose trust (Shoptegrity opinion, Grade A / A+). The format or experience may differ, and upfront cost may be higher.
