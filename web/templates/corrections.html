✍️ Corrections Policy

Found an Error? Tell Us.

We would rather be corrected than be wrong. Companies, employees, and readers can dispute any claim on this site by email.

How to Request a Correction

  1. Email corrections@shopteg.com with the page link and the specific figure or statement you believe is wrong.
  2. Include a source if you have one: a filing, a regulator record, an audited report, or a public statement.
  3. We aim to review each request and reply to the address that sent it.

What Happens Next

  • Factual errors (a wrong number, a wrong owner, an outdated filing) are corrected once verified.
  • Claims we cannot cite are marked Withheld: no cited source yet. Ownership claims without a cited source are withheld without waiting for a dispute.
  • Ratings and grades are our opinion under the published methodology. We aim to re-check the facts a rating relies on and update the rating when they change.

How to Read Our Data

  • "From SEC filings": the figure comes from the linked company filing for public companies.
  • "[source]": a specific fact (a count, a rate, an enforcement action) with a link to where it comes from.
  • "Withheld: no cited source yet": we have no citable source for that item, so it is not shown as a fact.
  • "Shoptegrity opinion": a grade or rating under our published methodology, not a statement of fact.
