The Local Services Ownership Ladder

The ladder is a Shoptegrity opinion of how to read ownership. A tier is shown for a business only when the directory holds a cited ownership record for it. Otherwise the tier is withheld.

Tier 1: Owner-Operated Local Shoptegrity opinion: highest retention

Single location, owner lives and works in the metro.

Tier 2: Local Multi-Location Shoptegrity opinion: high retention

2+ locations, all within one metro, privately owned by regional residents.

Tier 3: Local Franchisee Shoptegrity opinion: medium retention

Local operator under a national franchise brand. Where royalties and fees go is not shown here.

Tier 4: Regional Chain Shoptegrity opinion: medium-low retention

Headquarters in-state or adjacent state, not publicly traded, not private equity owned.

Tier 5: National Private Chain Shoptegrity opinion: low retention

National footprint, privately or family held.

Tier 6: Public / PE Rollup Shoptegrity opinion: lowest retention

Publicly traded, or owned by a private equity firm, according to a cited ownership record.
