Keep your money circulating in your community. We answer one question for local services, trades, and markets: who actually owns this business, and does the profit stay local?

The Local Services Ownership Ladder

Every service business is classified into exactly one tier based on public secretary of state records, franchise filings, and PE portfolio disclosures.

Tier 1: Owner-Operated Local Highest Retention

Single location, owner lives and works in the metro. Maximum local economic recirculation.

Tier 2: Local Multi-Location High Retention

2+ locations, all within one metro, privately owned by regional residents.

Tier 3: Local Franchisee Medium Retention

Local operator, national franchise brand. Royalties and tech fees leave the state.

Tier 4: Regional Chain Medium-Low

Headquarters in-state or adjacent state, not publicly traded, not private equity owned.

Tier 5: National Private Chain Low Retention

National footprint, privately or family held. Corporate profit leaves the region.

Tier 6: Public / PE Rollup Lowest Retention

Publicly traded, or acquired by private equity—including rollups operating under legacy family names.
