Four Comparisons to Make Before You Buy

General guidance for comparing options. It makes no claim about any named company.

  • Compare a credit union with your current bank: check fees, rates and membership rules, and look up each institution's registration before you decide.
  • Ask farms and co-ops how a purchase price is divided: compare their answer with your own receipt.
  • Ask who owns a local service business before you hire it: then look the business up in your state's business registry.
  • Ask your retirement plan administrator about proxy voting: find out which voting options the plan offers.
