## 1. Creative Brief & Executive Direction

* **Core Message:** The wealthy don't just own the products you buy; they own the jobs you work. Every time you spend money at a corporate chain or disguised private equity rollup, you fund the very machine that keeps your wages flat and your rent high. By building and patronizing peer-owned businesses, the bottom 90% can build a self-sustaining parallel economy that keeps wealth where it is produced.
* **Tone:** Clear, calm, authoritative, grounded, and ultimately electrifying with agency. No screeching, no conspiracy vibes, no academic jargon. Treat the audience like intelligent adults who are tired of being gaslit by corporate marketing.
* **Visual Style:** Hybrid of tactile, warm real-world footage (or cinematic AI live-action via Kling/Runway) and crisp, modern, minimalist motion graphics (Sankey diagrams, isometric neighborhood maps, transparent corporate facades).
* **Color Script:**
  * *Act I & II (The Extraction Trap):* Desaturated blues, corporate grays, harsh fluorescent office lighting, cold asphalt, sharp red/amber flow lines for extracted money.
  * *Act III & IV (The Parallel Circuit):* Warm golden-hour sunlight, tactile textures (wood, brick, soil, paper), deep forest green and glowing warm-amber flow lines showing recirculating wealth.
